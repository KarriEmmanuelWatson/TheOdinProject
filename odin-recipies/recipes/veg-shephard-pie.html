Vegetarian Shepherd's Pie

Description:

A comforting plant-based twist on the traditional pub classic, featuring a savory lentil and vegetable base beneath a layer of fluffy, golden mashed potatoes. It delivers all the rich, hearty satisfaction of the original recipe using wholesome, fiber-rich ingredients.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup brown lentils, rinsed
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups mixed frozen vegetables (peas, carrots, corn)
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 4 large potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until tender, then mash them with the butter, milk, salt, and pepper.
  2. In a separate pot, simmer the lentils in vegetable broth for about 20 minutes until tender.
  3. Sauté the onion and garlic in a skillet, then stir in the frozen vegetables, tomato paste, and cooked lentils.
  4. Spread the lentil and vegetable mixture into the bottom of a greased baking dish.
  5. Spread the mashed potatoes evenly over the top and bake at 400°F (200°C) for 25 minutes until the potato peaks are golden brown.
