Classic Beef Stew

Description:

A deeply savory, slow-simmered stew filled with tender chunks of beef, hearty root vegetables, and a rich, aromatic broth. Long cooking transforms tough cuts of meat into melt-in-your-mouth bites, making it the perfect comforting meal for chilly days.

Ingredients:

  • 2 pounds beef stew meat, cubed
  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cups beef broth
  • 1 cup red wine (optional)
  • 3 large carrots, sliced
  • 3 russet potatoes, cubed
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Toss the beef cubes in flour, salt, and pepper.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large pot and brown the beef on all sides, then remove the meat and set it aside.
  3. In the same pot, sauté the onion and garlic until soft.
  4. Pour in the beef broth and red wine, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom of the pot.
  5. Return the beef to the pot, add carrots, potatoes, and bay leaves, then cover and simmer on low heat for 1.5 to 2 hours until the beef is fork-tender.
