Creamy Stovetop Macaroni and Cheese

Description:

A rich and ultra-creamy stovetop mac and cheese made with a smooth cheddar cheese sauce that coats every single noodle perfectly. Skipping the oven keeps the pasta incredibly velvety, delivering ultimate comfort food in under thirty minutes.

Ingredients:

  • 8 ounces elbow macaroni
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 2 cups sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 1/2 teaspoon mustard powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Cook the macaroni in salted boiling water according to package instructions, then drain.
  2. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat, stir in the flour, and cook for 1 minute to create a roux.
  3. Slowly whisk in the milk, mustard powder, and garlic powder, simmering until the sauce thickens.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in the shredded cheddar cheese until completely melted and smooth.
  5. Stir the drained macaroni into the cheese sauce until well coated and serve immediately.
